## What it is

"Evaporated cane juice" is a sweetener made from sugar cane: the cane juice is evaporated and crystallized. In its May 2016 guidance for industry, FDA said the term is **not the common or usual name of any type of sweetener**, and that ingredients declared that way should instead be labeled as "sugar", optionally with a truthful descriptor such as "cane sugar". FDA's reasoning: the word "juice" suggests a fruit or vegetable juice and hides the fact that the ingredient is a sugar.

FDA guidance is nonbinding, so you may still see the old name on some labels.

## How US labels count it

It is sugar, so it is counted in both Total Sugars and Added Sugars.

## Sugar Depth's verdict: UGLY

Evaporated cane juice? That's sugar. Any added sugar makes a label UGLY under Sugar Depth's rules (rule U1), with no gram cutoff. The same goes for the names in the list above and dozens more. See the full list of [hidden sugar names](/hidden-sugar-names).
