## What it is

Monk fruit sweetener is an extract of the fruit of *Siraitia grosvenorii* Swingle, also called luo han guo. Its sweetness comes mainly from compounds called mogrosides. FDA describes it as 100 to 250 times sweeter than sugar, depending on mogroside content, and has not questioned GRAS notices for its use as a general-purpose and tabletop sweetener.

## How US labels count it

Monk fruit extract is not counted in Total Sugars or Added Sugars.

## Sugar Depth's verdict: OKAY

Plant sweeteners are OKAY under Sugar Depth's rules (rule O3). Many "monk fruit sweeteners" sold in bags are mostly [erythritol](/ingredients/erythritol) with a little monk fruit. Erythritol is OKAY too (rule O2), so the verdict stays OKAY unless something stricter is listed, because the worst ingredient decides.
