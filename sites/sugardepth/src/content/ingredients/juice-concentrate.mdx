## What it is

Juice concentrate is fruit or vegetable juice with most of the water removed. What's left is mostly the fruit's sugar, which is why it shows up in "no sugar added" style marketing for snacks, yogurts and bars.

## How US labels count it

FDA's definition of added sugars includes sugars from concentrated fruit or vegetable juices that are **in excess of what would be expected from the same volume of 100 percent juice** of the same type. FDA also lists exceptions: juice concentrated from 100% juice and sold to consumers (like frozen orange juice concentrate), concentrates used toward a product's total juice percentage, and the fruit component of jams, jellies and preserves made under their standards.

FDA does not count single-strength juice, fruit purees (including concentrated purees) or fruit pastes as added sugar.

## Sugar Depth's verdict

| Printed on the label | Sugar Depth |
|---|---|
| Apple juice concentrate, concentrated grape juice or fruit juice concentrate, sweetening another food | **Added sugar.** BAD above 4 g of added sugar per serving or when the grams can't be read{/* rules X1 and X2 */}, OKAY at 4 g or less{/* rule O1 */} |
| [Apple juice from concentrate](/ingredients/juice-from-concentrate), sweetening another food | Added sugar, same as above. Reconstituted to normal strength it is not added sugar. |
| [Fruit puree concentrate](/ingredients/fruit-puree-concentrate), sweetening another food | Added sugar, same as above |
| Any of the three in a product made only of fruit or vegetable juice or puree | Natural sugar. Allowed in CLEAN |
| [Apple juice, orange juice](/ingredients/fruit-or-vegetable-juice) (single strength) | Allowed in CLEAN |
| [Fruit puree](/ingredients/fruit-puree), applesauce, date paste | Allowed in CLEAN |

Sugar Depth matches juice wording with a small fixed grammar, so "apple juice concentrate" and "concentrated white grape juice" are both recognized without guessing.

Sugar Depth decides between the two from the rest of the ingredient list. A product counts as made only of juice when everything else on the list is fruit, vegetables, juice, puree, water, acids, vitamins and minerals, preservatives, colors, gums, salt or flavors.

Sugar's other costume with "juice" in the name is [evaporated cane juice](/ingredients/evaporated-cane-juice).
