## What it is

Xylitol is a sugar alcohol (polyol). FDA permits sugar alcohols as sugar substitutes and lists xylitol among them, used in foods such as sugar-free candies, cookies and chewing gum. "Birch xylitol" names where it was made from, not a different ingredient.

## How US labels count it

Like other sugar alcohols, xylitol is not part of Total Sugars or Added Sugars. A label may show it on a voluntary "Sugar alcohol" line, and when it is the only sugar alcohol present the line may simply say "Xylitol" (21 CFR 101.9(c)(6)(iv)).

## Sugar Depth's verdict: UGLY

Sugar Depth's rules put xylitol and [erythritol](/ingredients/erythritol) in UGLY (rule U4). Other sugar alcohols such as sorbitol, maltitol, mannitol, isomalt and lactitol are BAD (rule B1). This is a product preference, not a medical finding.
