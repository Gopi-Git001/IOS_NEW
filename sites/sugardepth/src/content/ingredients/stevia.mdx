## What it is

Stevia sweeteners are steviol glycosides, such as rebaudioside A (Reb A), rebaudioside M and stevioside, taken from the leaves of the stevia plant or made by fermentation. FDA describes them as 200 to 400 times sweeter than table sugar. FDA has not questioned GRAS notices for high-purity steviol glycosides (at least 95% pure). FDA does not consider stevia leaf or crude stevia extracts GRAS, and they are not permitted as sweeteners in the US.

## How US labels count it

Steviol glycosides are not sugars and are not counted in Total Sugars or Added Sugars.

## Sugar Depth's verdict: BAD

Sugar Depth's rules call plant sweeteners BAD (rule B2): better than added sugar or an artificial sweetener, but still a sweetener. Stevia never overrides a worse ingredient. Stevia is frequently blended with [erythritol](/ingredients/erythritol), and that blend is UGLY, because erythritol is UGLY and every ingredient inside a blend is checked.
