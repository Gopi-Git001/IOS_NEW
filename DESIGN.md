# Sugar Depth design system

One system for the scanner app (`src/`) and the marketing site (`sites/sugardepth`). Tagline:
"Read the fine print." The current verdict direction is **The Verdict, glossy crystal**.

## Current verdict surfaces

- Warm editorial paper, faint grid and soft light beams on the site; a warm translucent scrim over the captured photo in the app.
- A giant glossy black verdict word, a fading mirror reflection and a small coloured glossy bead as its full stop.
- A thick tilted rectangular glass slab in the verdict colour: visible edge, inset rim, diagonal inner streaks, sheen and glints, with a soft coloured floor caustic. Never substitute faceted gem shapes.
- A hairline rule and Newsreader italic reason underneath; Libre Franklin carries the verdict and UI.
- Brand fills stay CLEAN `#5be49b`, BAD `#ffc24d`, UGLY `#ff7a66`; darker `cleanInk`, `badInk`, `uglyInk` tokens carry readable coloured text on light paper.
- Site verdict preview uses native HTML radios and CSS only. Reduced motion disables the sheen; app reduced motion also disables the sheet spring and reading shimmer.
- Existing camera controls, glossary, menu and profile retain their dark glass. Only history verdict badges adopt the slab material. Verdict wording, rules, data flow and interactions are unchanged.

The sections below document the retained dark-glass foundation, not the current light verdict surfaces.

Below-hero editorial media pairs Higgsfield stills generated on 2026-11-09 with `gpt_image_2_6`
(label magnifier, sugar aliases, three tinted slabs and a sugar specimen) with HyperFrames loops.
Responsive AVIF/WebP stills are lazy-loaded in rounded paper-toned frames; loops play only while
visible, pause when the page is hidden and respect reduced motion. The homepage hero stays unchanged.

## Principles
- Glass over light. Every surface is a frosted sheet over a dark ambient backdrop: a deep blue-black base
  with three soft pools of light (teal top left, indigo right, amber bottom left) for the glass to refract.
- One colored word. White type carries everything; color appears only on the verdict word, the
  ingredient names, their tags and the stats bar.
- Rules, not boxes. Structure inside a sheet comes from thin white rules: a 2 pt strong rule under titles,
  1 pt between blocks, hairlines between rows, a 2 pt rule to close the list.
- The cube stays. The glossy sugar cube character appears with every verdict (clean, bad, ugly, scanning, idle).

## Tokens (brand/tokens.json)
`brand/tokens.json` is the only place brand colors are defined. The app imports it in `src/theme.ts`;
the site gets `sites/sugardepth/src/styles/tokens.css`, generated by `npm run tokens`. CI fails if the
CSS is out of date (`node scripts/sync-tokens.mjs --check`). Change a color there, never in a component.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper / background | #06080D | Base under the ambient light |
| sheet | rgba(16,20,30,0.62) | Large frosted sheets: result, glossary, site panels and CTA |
| paperRaised | rgba(255,255,255,0.07) | Groups, inputs, chips on a sheet |
| ink | #F4F6FB | Text and strong rules |
| inkMuted | 74% ink | Secondary text |
| inkFaint | 60% ink | Footers, version line, captions |
| rule / hairline | 34% / 12% white | Rules between blocks / rows |
| onInk | #0A0D14 | Text on the bright primary button |
| ugly | #FF7A66 | UGLY verdict, added-sugar names, danger |
| bad | #FFC24D | BAD verdict, sweetener names |
| clean | #5BE49B | CLEAN verdict, natural-sugar names |
| ambient.* | see file | Base gradient and the three glow colors |

Verdict colors are at least 7:1 on #0B0F18.

## Glass
- Frost: `backdrop-filter: blur(28px) saturate(170%)`, a 1 px border at 16% white, an inset top highlight
  at 28% white and a soft drop shadow (`frost()` in `src/theme.ts`, `--frost-*` in the site CSS).
- Native (iOS) uses Liquid Glass on floating controls (profile capsule, shutter, hint, reading pill, back
  button) and the translucent `sheet` fill elsewhere.

## Type
- Libre Franklin 900 for the wordmark, titles, the verdict word and grams; 400 to 800 for rows, labels and buttons.
- Newsreader italic for the voice: tagline, the "why" line, glossary explanations, empty states.

## Components
- Wordmark: "Sugar Depth" in Libre Franklin 900 with the cube mark; the mark glows in the verdict color.
- Camera frame: hairline box labeled "Ingredients", with dark scrims top and bottom so white text holds over any photo.
- Verdict Facts panel: title, strong rule, "Your label / per ingredient list", verdict word, italic reason,
  then tappable ingredient rows with outlined tags (SUGAR, SWEETENER, NATURAL).
- Primary button: a bright pill (ink fill, onInk text). Secondary: a hairline pill with ink text.
- Site only: film bands hold the HyperFrames videos (`public/media`) and real app screenshots (`public/app`).

## Accessibility
- Body text at least 4.5:1 on the darkest sheet; verdict colors at least 7:1.
- All motion respects reduced motion (app: `useReducedMotion`; site: `prefers-reduced-motion` and the data-motion helper).
